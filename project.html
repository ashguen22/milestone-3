I am planning on putting in different variables that directly relate with each other into a graphic that will help people better understand their correlation. For example, the relationship between batting average and striekout numbers is one that many people do not think about, but I will explore further. My project will show the success of players based on this information, so I will eventually create a rankings system that shows this based on the statistics provided. I hope to make it interactive as well, and show the players’ pictures and other interesting facts about them as well.
